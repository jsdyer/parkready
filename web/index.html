Know what this check covers

This is a dated copy of the BC Parks public catalogue, refreshed hourly. It does not cover every municipal, regional or national park, live road conditions, camping availability or unreported hazards. No listed advisory is not an “open” or “safe” determination.

BC Parks says advisories are normally updated Monday–Friday, 8:30 am–4:30 pm, excluding statutory holidays. Check the official advisory page, EmergencyInfoBC and DriveBC before travel. Planning dates follow the BC Parks date convention (UTC−7).

Searches, date choices and favourites are processed locally. Saving a park stores its public park ID on this device. Official links and map links open external sites only when clicked.
